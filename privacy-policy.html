Privacy Policy — My Band

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Last updated: October 1, 2026
My Band is a personal, non-commercial wellness tool built and used by a single individual developer to read their own Huawei Health data (sleep, heart rate, and activity) and generate a daily wellness summary. This policy explains what data is accessed, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Who we are

My Band is an individual, personal-use application developed by an independent developer registered as an Individual Developer with the Huawei Developer Program. My Band is not a commercial product and is not distributed to the public.

2. What data we access

With explicit authorization granted through your Huawei ID, My Band accesses the following data types from Huawei Health Kit:

  • Sleep data — sleep stages (deep, light, REM, awake), total sleep duration, and sleep score.
  • Heart rate data — resting and continuous heart rate readings.
  • Activity data — daily step count and workout session details (type, duration, calories burned).

My Band only reads data. It does not write, modify, or delete any data in your Huawei Health account.

3. How we use your data

The data described above is used exclusively to:

  • Generate a daily personal wellness report summarizing sleep quality, heart rate trends, and activity levels.
  • Produce personalized, non-medical recommendations to help improve sleep and general activity habits over time.
  • Build a private historical view of the user's own health trends across days, weeks, and months.

Your data is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties, and is not used for any purpose beyond generating your own personal report.

4. Data storage and security

Data retrieved from Huawei Health Kit is stored only for the purpose of generating the personal wellness report and is kept under the account owner's control. Reasonable technical measures (encrypted storage and access tokens, restricted access) are used to protect the data. Access tokens are refreshed securely using OAuth 2.0 and are never shared with third parties.

5. Data sharing

My Band does not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your health data to any third party, advertiser, or external service, except:

  • Where required to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • With Huawei's own systems, strictly as required to authenticate and retrieve the data you have authorized.

6. Your rights and control

As the account owner, you may at any time:

  • Revoke My Band's access to your Huawei Health data from the Huawei Health app (Me > Privacy management) or from your Huawei ID account settings.
  • Request deletion of any locally stored data generated by My Band.
  • Stop using the application at any time with no impact on your Huawei Health account or device.

7. Children's privacy

My Band is intended for use by adults managing their own personal health data and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in functionality or applicable data scopes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
